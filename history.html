History of Bihar's Education

Ancient Times

Chandragupta Maurya

Chandragupta I aur Chandragupta II Gupta dynasty ke great rulers the.
Unka time India ke liye ek golden era maana jaata hai.
Unhone Bihar mein education aur art ko promote kiya.
Bade universities jaise Nalanda aur Vikramshila unke time mein develop hue.
Unke era mein logon ne mathematics, astrology, medicine aur philosophy ki knowledge li.

Kumaragupta

Kumaragupta I Gupta vansh ke ek aur pramukh shashak the. Unhone hi Nalanda Vishwavidyalay ki sthapna ki thi. Yahan desh-videsh se chhatra padhne aate the. Bihar ko ek shiksha ke kendra banane mein unka yogdan mahatvapurn tha.

Acharya Chanakya

Acharya Chanakya, jinhone Arthashastra likha, prachin Bharat ke ek mahan vidwan the. Unhone Takshashila Vishwavidyalay mein padhaaya aur education system ko majboot banaya. Unka yogdan na keval rajniti mein tha balki shiksha ke kshetra mein bhi atyant mahatvapurn tha. Unhone Chandragupta Maurya ko shikshit karke ek shaktishaali samrajya ki sthapna mein sahayata ki.

Dharampal

Dharampal ek prasiddh itihaskar the jinhone British documents ke madhyam se prachin Bharatiya shiksha pranali ko duniya ke saamne rakha.
Unhone bataya ki Bihar jaise rajyon mein angrezon ke aane se pehle lokvidya aur pathshalayen kaafi vikshit thi.
Unka kaam Bharat ke shiksha itihaas ko samajhne mein ek naye drishtikon deta hai.
Unhone education system ki asli tasveer duniya ke saamne prastut ki.
