History of Bihar's Education

Ancient Times

Chandragupta Maurya

Chandragupta I aur Chandragupta II Gupta dynasty ke great rulers the.
Unka time India ke liye ek golden era maana jaata hai.
Unhone Bihar mein education aur art ko promote kiya.
Bade universities jaise Nalanda aur Vikramshila unke time mein develop hue.
Unke era mein logon ne mathematics, astrology, medicine aur philosophy ki knowledge li.

Kumaragupta

Kumaragupta I Gupta vansh ke ek aur pramukh shashak the.
Unhone hi Nalanda Vishwavidyalay ki sthapna ki thi.
Yahan desh-videsh se chhatra padhne aate the.
Bihar ko ek shiksha ke kendra banane mein unka yogdan mahatvapurn tha.

Acharya Nagarjuna

Acharya Nagarjuna ek prasiddh gyaani aur darshanik the.
Ve Nalanda Vishwavidyalay mein shikshak the.
Unka yogdan purane Bharat ke darshan aur tark shastra mein smaraniya hai.

Dharmapala (Pala Vansh)

Dharmapala Pala vansh ke ek pramukh raja the.
Unhone Vikramshila Vishwavidyalay ki sthapna ki thi.
Inka uddeshya uchch shiksha aur bauddh gyaan ko badhava dena tha.

📜 Evolution of Education in Bihar - Timeline

  • 🕰 Ancient Era: Universities like Nalanda and Vikramshila were established.
  • ⚔ Medieval Period: Education declined due to invasions.
  • 📚 British Era: Formal schooling introduced.
  • 🎓 Post-Independence: More schools and colleges were opened.

  • Chinese scholar Hiuen Tsang came to study at Nalanda.
