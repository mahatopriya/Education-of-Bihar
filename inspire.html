Inspiring Stories from Bihar

1.dr bhimrao ambedkar

Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar, also known as Babasaheb, was an Indian jurist, economist, politician, and social reformer. He was born on April 14, 1891, and died on December 6, 1956. He is widely recognized as the architect of the Indian Constitution and is considered a pivotal figure in India's independence movement and social reform efforts.

2. Sudha Varghese Prerna School

Sudha Ma'am dedicated her life to teaching Dalit girls in Bihar. She started the Prerna School for Musahar girls and received the Padma Shri for her work.Sudha Ma'am dedicated her life to educating Dalit and Musahar girls in Bihar, one of the most marginalized communities in India. She started Prerna School in Danapur to provide free education, life skills, and a safe environment for these girls who were otherwise deprived of basic rights.

3. Village Teachers Teaching Without Resources

In the remote villages of Bihar, many dedicated teachers continue to teach without classrooms, electricity, or proper materials. They often run schools under trees, in open fields, or borrowed community spaces, using simple blackboards and chalk.Despite the lack of resources, their commitment to children’s education remains unshaken. .

4. Lalita Prasad River Island Teacher

Lalita Prasad is a remarkable teacher who crosses a river by boat every single day to reach a remote island village in Bihar, where children have no access to formal schools. With no proper road or bridge, the journey is often risky — especially during floods or heavy rains — but her unshakable commitment to education keeps her going.

5.Bindeshwar Pathak Sulabh Movement

Dr. Bindeshwar Pathak was a visionary social reformer who founded the Sulabh Sanitation and Social Reform Movement in 1970. His mission was to ensure cleanliness, dignity, and social equality by ending the inhuman practice of manual scavenging, especially in rural areas of Bihar and other states..
