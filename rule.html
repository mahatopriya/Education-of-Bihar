Bihar Education Rules and Acts

Empowering Future Generations Through Education

Official Website of the Education Department, Bihar

Bihar follows the Right to Education Act (2009).

Every child aged 6 to 14 years has the right to free and compulsory education.

Private schools must reserve 25% seats for poor children under RTE.

Bihar School Examination ActControls and manages board exams through Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).

The department manages:Primary Education (Classes 1–5)

Secondary Education (Classes 6–10)

Senior Secondary Education (Classes 11–12)

Higher Education (Colleges & Universities)

About the Education Department

Mission: Provide quality and accessible education to all citizens.

History: Established in 1947, evolving to meet modern challenges.

This board conducts Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Intermediate) exams.

It also manages D.El.Ed (Diploma in Elementary Education) and Teachers’ Eligibility Test (BTET).

Major bodies under this department include:Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB)

State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT)

Bihar Education Project Council (BEPC)

Key initiatives run by the department:Mid-Day Meal Scheme

Mukhyamantri Balika/Balak Cycle Yojana

Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme

Scholarship Programs for SC/ST/OBC/Minority students

Key Education Rules and Regulations

Comprehensive Coverage: Rules govern primary to higher education sectors.

School Administration: Guidelines for curriculum and teacher appointments.

Resources: Downloadable PDFs of official government notifications.

Right to Education: Free and compulsory education for all children.

Right to Education (RTE) Act, 2009Provides free and compulsory education to all children aged 6 to 14 years.

Private schools must reserve 25% seats for underprivileged children.

School Structure and LevelsPrimary (Class 1–5), Upper Primary (Class 6–8), Secondary (Class 9–10), and Senior Secondary (Class 11–12).

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB)Governs the curriculum, exams, and evaluation for classes 10 and 12.

Mid-Day Meal SchemeProvides free meals to students up to Class 8 in government and aided schools.

Encourages school attendance and improves nutrition.

Student Welfare Schemes Free textbooks, uniforms, and bicycles for students.

Scholarships for SC, ST, OBC, and economically weaker sections.

Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme for higher education loans.

Teacher Eligibility and TrainingTeachers must qualify BTET/CTET and complete D.El.Ed or B.Ed.

Regular teacher training and performance monitoring are mandatory.

Girl Child Education ProgramsFinancial aid, free cycles, and incentives for girl students.

Programs like Kanya Utthan Yojana promote higher education among girls.

Digital Learning InitiativesIntroduction of smart classes, digital libraries, and online learning in government schools.

Scholarship Programs

Scholarship Types: Multiple schemes for various education levels.

Eligibility & Applications: Clear criteria and step-by-step process.

DBT Disbursement: Timely payments via Direct Benefit Transfer.

Funding: ₹500 Crore disbursed in 2023.

Eligibility Criteria Must Be FulfilledStudent must belong to the category (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) as mentioned in the scheme. – Must be a resident of Bihar.

Minimum Educational QualificationMust be studying in recognized school/college/institution.

Class and marks requirements vary as per scholarship.

Income Limit RequiredFamily annual income must be below the limit (e.g., ₹2.5 lakh for SC/ST, ₹1.5 lakh for OBC).

One Scholarship per Student Rule A student cannot apply for more than one scholarship for the same academic year.

Valid Documents Are MandatoryAadhaar card, income certificate, caste certificate, bank account, mark sheets, etc.

Online Application OnlyAll scholarships must be applied through the official Bihar scholarship portals.

Timely Application Submission – Applications must be submitted before the deadline announced every year.

Regular Attendance & Academic ProgressStudent must maintain attendance and pass the exams to continue receiving the scholarship.
