Rules and Acts

  1. Education is a fundamental right for every child.
  2. Primary education is free and compulsory in Bihar.
  3. Secondary education includes both academic and skill development.
  4. The state promotes girls' education through scholarships.
  5. Teachers must be professionally qualified.
  6. School infrastructure should be clean and safe.
  7. Mid-day meal scheme is mandatory in primary schools.
  8. Higher education policies focus on employability.
  9. Technology integration is encouraged in teaching.
  10. Regular audits ensure education quality in institutions.
